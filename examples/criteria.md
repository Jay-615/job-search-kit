# Job Search Criteria

*Example for a fictional person, Sam Chen: 8 years in e-commerce product management, based in Seattle. Setup writes your real version to `my-search/criteria.md`.*

## Roles
- Senior Product Manager (preferred role)
- Product Manager
- Principal Product Manager
- Lead Product Manager

## Title family
Product Manager and close variants (Senior PM, Lead PM, Principal PM, Group PM). Not Product Marketing Manager, Engineering Manager, Account Manager or Customer Success Manager unless the job description clearly describes product-management work.

## Locations
- Home base: Seattle, WA (within 30 miles)
- Remote (US-based): yes
- Hybrid with an office in the Seattle area: yes

## Seniority
- Target: Senior individual contributor (Senior, Lead, Principal)
- Also OK, scored lower: mid-level, Director
- Reject: VP and above, Head of, Chief, Associate, Junior, Intern

## Pay floor
- $140,000 per year, or $80 per hour
- Only applied when a posting shows its pay. Postings with no pay listed are kept.

## Job types
- Full-time
- Contract
- Contract-to-hire

## Industries (a soft signal in scoring, not a filter)
- Strong match: e-commerce (marketplace, DTC, retail platforms), tech (SaaS, consumer apps), fintech (payments, banking, lending)
- Partial match: logistics and fulfillment, subscription commerce, adtech tied to commerce
- Avoid: none
